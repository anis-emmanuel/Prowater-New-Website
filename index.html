The Process

Up and running
in 7 simple steps

We handle everything end-to-end. Your society just says yes — and within days, clean water is flowing into every subscribed home.

01

Site Assessment

Our engineer visits your society, maps the building layout, and evaluates water source and infrastructure needs — all in a single visit.

Free visit1 day
02

Feasibility Check

Our engineers design the optimal centralised RO system for your building — sized exactly for your floors, flat count, and daily water demand.

Custom design2–3 days
03

Installation

Rooftop RO plant installed and 16mm flexi pipes run through building ducts floor by floor — minimal disruption, usually done in 3–5 days.

3–5 daysZero disruption
04

Quality Testing

Every parameter verified before go-live — TDS, pH, hardness, and bacterial levels all tested and certified safe. Visible live in the app.

TDS certifiedApp verified
05

Resident Sign-ups

Each household chooses their own plan individually — no society-wide mandate. Residents can join, pause, or cancel completely independently.

Individual choiceNo pressure
06

Dispenser Fitted

A smart Wi-Fi IoT dispenser is installed in your kitchen. Choose normal-only or add instant hot water. Monitors quality live, 24/7.

IoT enabledHot + Cold option
✓

Go Live!

Pure water on tap from day one. ProWater monitors quality remotely, maintains the system, and handles all servicing — forever.
